# Excavator operation basics

## Controls (standard ISO pattern)
- **Left joystick:** forward/back moves the stick out and in; left/right swings the upper structure.
- **Right joystick:** forward/back lowers and raises the boom; left/right curls and dumps the bucket.
- **Travel levers/pedals:** push both forward to travel forward. Check which way the tracks face
  (the travel motors are at the rear); if the blade is in front of you, the controls are reversed.
- **Hydraulic lockout lever:** must be in LOCKED before leaving the seat.

## Speed limits and travel
Tracked excavators travel slowly (around 3–6 km/h). Travel with the bucket about 30–40 cm above the
ground and the boom lowered, and use low travel speed on slopes, rough ground and near people.
Exceeding the machine's safe travel speed is recorded as unsafe operation.

## Digging a trench
1. Position the machine so the tracks are square to the trench and the travel motors are at the rear.
2. Dig in layers of about one bucket depth, starting at the far end and pulling towards the machine.
3. Keep the spoil pile at least 1 metre back from the trench edge.
4. Before digging, confirm underground services (power, gas, water, fibre) are marked. Hand-dig near
   marked services.

## Working on slopes
Travel straight up or down slopes, never across them. Keep the bucket low and pointing downhill when
descending. Do not swing a loaded bucket downhill on a slope, as it can tip the machine.

## Smooth operation
Harsh, jerky movements (sudden starts and stops of swing, boom or travel) wear pins, bushings and the
swing gear, spill material, and increase tip-over risk. The app counts harsh events from the phone's
motion sensors; four or more in one session is treated as unsafe operation.
