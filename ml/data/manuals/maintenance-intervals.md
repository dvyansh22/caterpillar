# Maintenance intervals

## Service interval summary
Intervals are counted in engine hours (shown on the hour meter or in the app).
- **Construction machines:** full service every **500 hours**.
- **Mining machines:** full service every **400 hours**, because of heavier duty cycles, dust and
  longer shifts.
The app marks a machine as *maintenance due* when hours since last service pass these limits, when
any fault code is active, or when engine temperature goes above 105°C.

## Every 10 hours or daily
Walkaround inspection (see *pre-start-inspection.md*), engine oil, coolant and hydraulic levels,
drain water from the fuel water separator, empty the air pre-cleaner, grease pins and bushings on
excavator front linkages and loader linkages, and check the seatbelt.

## Every 250 hours
Engine oil and filter change (first change; then at every 500-hour service unless oil analysis allows
longer), check fan and alternator belts, check battery terminals, take oil samples, and check the
track tension or tire pressures.

## Every 500 hours (construction) / 400 hours (mining)
Engine oil and filter, primary and secondary fuel filters, hydraulic return filter, air filter
primary element (or earlier if the restriction indicator trips), swing drive and final drive oil
level checks, radiator core cleaning, and a full inspection of brakes and steering.

## Every 2,000 hours
Hydraulic oil change (or per oil analysis), coolant test and top-up of coolant extender, final drive
and swing drive oil change, and valve lash check.

## Oil analysis
Taking an oil sample at every service lets the technician spot internal wear early (for example,
rising iron or silicon levels mean wear or dust entry). Label each sample with the machine ID and
engine hours.
