# Pre-start walkaround inspection

Do this inspection at the start of every shift, before starting the engine. It takes about
10 minutes. Record any defect and report it before operating.

## Walkaround checklist — all machines
Walk around the machine clockwise, starting at the cab steps.
1. **Leaks:** look under the machine for fresh oil, fuel, coolant or hydraulic fluid.
2. **Fluid levels:** engine oil (dipstick), coolant (expansion tank), hydraulic oil (sight glass),
   fuel level, and DEF/AdBlue if fitted.
3. **Hoses and cylinders:** no cracks, chafing, bulges or wet fittings.
4. **Air filter indicator:** not in the red zone.
5. **Guards and steps:** handrails and steps clean, secure and free of mud and grease.
6. **Lights, horn, travel alarm and mirrors/cameras:** working and clean.
7. **Fire extinguisher:** present, charged and within inspection date.
8. **Seatbelt:** latches and releases correctly, webbing not cut or frayed.
9. **Work area:** check for overhead power lines, trenches, soft ground and people before moving.

## Additional checks — tracked machines (excavator, dozer)
- Track sag roughly equal on both sides; no missing track bolts or cracked shoes.
- Undercarriage free of packed mud and rocks, which increase wear and tension.
- Bucket teeth and cutting edges secure; replace teeth worn past the wear line.
- Swing bearing and pins greased according to the lubrication chart.

## Additional checks — wheeled machines and haul trucks
- Tires: no cuts, bulges or exposed cords; check cold pressure with a gauge.
- Wheel nuts: none missing or loose (look for rust streaks around the nut).
- Brakes: test the service brake and parking brake before leaving the parking area.
- Haul trucks: check the body is fully lowered and the body-up indicator is off; check the
  retarder and steering for correct response at low speed.

## If a check fails
Do not operate a machine with a safety defect (brakes, steering, seatbelt, lights, alarms, or major
leaks). Tag the machine "Do Not Operate", report the defect in the app, and inform your supervisor.
Minor defects may be recorded and repaired at the next service if the supervisor agrees.
