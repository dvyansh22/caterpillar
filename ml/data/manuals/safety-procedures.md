# Operator safety procedures

## Seatbelt use
Wear the seatbelt at all times while the engine is running, including short moves and while idling.
In a rollover, the seatbelt keeps the operator inside the protective structure (ROPS); most rollover
deaths happen to operators who were not belted in and were thrown from the cab.
- Fasten the belt before starting the engine and keep it fastened until the engine is off.
- The belt should sit low across the hips, not across the stomach.
- The app flags a session as seatbelt non-compliant if the belt is unfastened for more than 10% of
  moving time.

## Mounting and dismounting
Always use three points of contact (two hands and one foot, or two feet and one hand). Face the
machine. Never jump from the cab or steps, and never mount or dismount a moving machine.

## Working near people and other machines (proximity)
- Know the machine's blind spots and swing radius. Nobody should be inside the swing radius of an
  excavator while it is working.
- Make eye contact with ground workers before moving, and agree hand signals with the spotter.
- A proximity warning from the app means a worker or machine is inside the warning zone. Stop
  movement, locate the person, and continue only when the zone is clear.
- Three or more proximity warnings in one session are treated as unsafe operation.

## Fatigue
Fatigue slows reaction time as much as alcohol. Warning signs are frequent yawning, heavy eyelids,
drifting attention, and missing signals.
- Take a break of at least 10–15 minutes every 2 hours of operation.
- If the app shows a high fatigue score (above 0.7), stop in a safe place, rest, and inform the
  supervisor. Do not continue operating until you feel alert.
- On night shifts and long mining shifts, fatigue risk is highest in the early morning hours.

## Parking and shutdown
1. Park on level ground, away from edges, overhead lines and traffic routes.
2. Lower the bucket, blade or body fully to the ground.
3. Engage the parking brake and move the hydraulic lockout lever to LOCKED.
4. Let the engine idle for 3–5 minutes to cool the turbocharger, then shut it off.
5. Remove the key when leaving the machine.

## Emergency and SOS
In an emergency, stop the machine safely if you can, then long-press the SOS button in the app.
The SOS is broadcast over Bluetooth to nearby phones even without mobile signal, and relayed to the
supervisor when any phone reaches the network. If there is a fire, shut down the engine, use the
extinguisher only if it is safe, and move away upwind.

## Lockout before maintenance
Before any inspection inside guards or under raised implements: lower all implements, shut down the
engine, remove the key, lock the hydraulic control, and attach a "Do Not Operate" tag. Never work
under a raised bucket, blade or dump body unless it is mechanically supported.
