# Dashboard warning lights and alarms

Warning indicators have three levels. The action depends on the level, not the colour alone.

## Level 1 — information (amber indicator)
The machine needs attention soon but can keep working. Examples: air filter restriction, fuel level
low, service due. Finish the current task and report it.

## Level 2 — change operation (amber indicator and alarm)
Change how you are operating to prevent damage. Examples: coolant or hydraulic oil temperature high,
low tire pressure. Reduce load or speed at once, and follow the matching fault code procedure.

## Level 3 — stop safely (red indicator, flashing, continuous alarm)
Stop the machine in a safe place and shut the engine down as soon as it is safe. Examples: engine oil
pressure low, brake pressure low, severe overheating. Do not restart until a technician clears it.

## Common indicators
- **Engine oil pressure (oil can):** Level 3. Shut down immediately; continuing can destroy the engine.
- **Coolant temperature (thermometer in liquid):** Level 2 when high; Level 3 above 110°C with alarm.
  See ENGINE_OVERHEAT.
- **Hydraulic oil temperature:** Level 2. Reduce work rate and let the oil cool; check the cooler for
  debris.
- **Air filter (filter symbol):** Level 1. See AIR_FILTER_RESTRICTED.
- **Brake pressure / brake wear (circle with brackets):** Level 3 for low pressure; Level 2 for wear.
- **Seatbelt (person with belt):** reminder that the seatbelt is not fastened.
- **Charging system (battery):** alternator not charging; finish the task and report it.
